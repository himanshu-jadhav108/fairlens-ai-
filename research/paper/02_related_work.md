## 2.1 Algorithmic Bias Mitigation in Machine Learning
- Pre-processing interventions: Feature projection, reweighting, and representation learning (e.g., Feldman et al., 2015).
- In-processing reductions: Constrained optimization and game-theoretic minimax formulations (e.g., Agarwal et al., 2018).
- Post-processing adjustments: Decoupled classification and group-specific decision thresholding (e.g., Hardt et al., 2016).

## 2.2 Model Explainability and Cooperative Game Theory
- Shapley additive explanations (SHAP) and local feature attribution (Lundberg & Lee, 2017).
- TreeExplainer, LinearExplainer, and attribution stability in tabular domains.

## 2.3 Fairness–Performance Trade-Offs
- The theoretical and empirical Pareto frontier between accuracy and fairness metrics (e.g., Menon & Williamson, 2018; Corbett-Davies et al., 2017).

## 2.4 Explainability Under Fairness Constraints
- Prior studies investigating the interplay between fairness interventions and explanation fidelity/stability.
