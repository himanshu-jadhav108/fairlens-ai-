## 5. EVALUATOR IMPLEMENTATION INTEGRITY

### 5.1 Evaluator Architecture
The evaluation framework in `research/faithfulness/` decomposes generated text into atomic claims and evaluates them deterministically:
1. **Clause Splitting & Boundary Isolation:** Sentence and clause regex segmentation (`CLAUSE_DELIMITER_PATTERN`) prevents metric bleed across complex compound sentences.
2. **State-Aware Candidate Filtering:** The evaluator detects temporal/state context (`baseline`, `mitigated`, `absolute_change`, `relative_change`), restricting ground-truth matching to the correct state pool to prevent cross-state false positives.
3. **Tolerance Formulation:**
   $$\text{Match} \iff |v_{\text{extracted}} - v_{\text{true}}| \le 0.015 \quad\lor\quad \frac{|v_{\text{extracted}} - v_{\text{true}}|}{|v_{\text{true}}|} \le 0.05$$
   This tolerates reasonable rounding (e.g., $0.1895 \to 0.190$) while penalizing hallucinations and quantitative drift.
4. **Directional & Semantic Interpretation:**
   - Separates mathematical direction (`increase`/`decrease`) from domain fairness interpretation (`improvement`/`degradation`).
   - Routes subjective or qualitative magnitude terms (e.g., *"substantially"*, *"drastically"*) to `UNDETERMINABLE` rather than assigning arbitrary automated labels.
5. **Unsupported Claim Detection:**
   - Scans for unevidenced causal assertions (*"directly causes"*), absolute fairness declarations (*"completely fair"*, *"guarantees zero bias"*), and unwarranted overgeneralizations.
6. **Denominator Rules:**
   - **Numerical Faithfulness:**
     $$\text{Faithfulness}_{\text{num}} = \frac{N_{\text{SUPPORTED}}}{N_{\text{SUPPORTED}} + N_{\text{UNSUPPORTED}}}$$
     `UNDETERMINABLE` claims (orphan numbers with no metric anchor) are excluded from the denominator.
   - **Directional Faithfulness:**
     $$\text{Faithfulness}_{\text{dir}} = \frac{D_{\text{SUPPORTED}}}{D_{\text{SUPPORTED}} + D_{\text{UNSUPPORTED}}}$$
     Conditions with 0 directional claims are evaluated as `None` (`N/A`) and excluded from pairwise directional tests.
   - **Unsupported Claim Rate:**
     $$\text{Rate}_{\text{unsup}} = \frac{C_{\text{UNSUPPORTED}}}{C_{\text{TOTAL}}}$$
     Denominator is the total claim count across all categories.

### 6.3 Scientific Implications of the Statistical Reconciliation
1. **Hypothesis 1 (Numerical Degradation):** Strongly **CONFIRMED** ($p = 1.34 \times 10^{-7}, d = -1.097$). Foundation models exhibit substantial, statistically significant numerical degradation compared to deterministic template generation.
2. **Hypothesis 2 (Unsupported Claims):** Strongly **CONFIRMED** ($p = 1.20 \times 10^{-6}, d = 0.976$). Gemini generates unsupported claims at a mean rate of $8.5\%$, whereas templates produce $0.0\%$.
3. **Hypothesis 3 (Directional vs Numerical Robustness):** Directional faithfulness in Gemini ($94.2\%$) is indeed superior to numerical faithfulness ($88.1\%$). However, on the true 36-condition matrix ($23$ evaluable pairs), the difference between Gemini and Template directionality is **not statistically significant** ($p = 0.198$). The paper must state this transparently and must **NOT** claim statistically significant directional degradation based on the contaminated $p = 0.0396$.
